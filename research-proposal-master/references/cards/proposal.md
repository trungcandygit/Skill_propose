# Tier 1 role card: Research proposal (đề cương nghiên cứu, PhD/thesis proposal, grant, pre-analysis plan)

Load this card **in addition to** core.md and your role card whenever the task is writing, auditing or revising a research proposal (đề cương nghiên cứu sinh, đề cương luận văn/luận án, thuyết minh đề tài, grant proposal, pre-analysis plan). Every rule in core.md still applies; "manuscript" there means the proposal here, and "reviewer comments" include comments from the advisor, the admissions or funding committee, and self-review. Rule IDs are `P-xx`; cite them in findings (e.g. "FAIL MAJOR P-23"). Main session: read in full before writing any proposal text. Seat 1 checks sections A–C, F–H; Seat 2 checks sections D–E and the consistency matrix (P-71).

## A. Intake: the call, the committee and the facts

- **P-01** Before writing, collect and read in full the call or regulation the proposal answers (thông báo tuyển sinh, quy chế đào tạo tiến sĩ, mẫu đề cương của trường, chương trình hoặc quỹ tài trợ, nghị quyết hoặc danh mục bài toán), the required template, page/word limits, mandatory sections and review criteria; list them as a compliance table (requirement | source with article/page | where the proposal meets it). Why: a missing required section or a broken limit is rejected before anyone reads the science. → `skills/research-grants/SKILL.md#formatting-mistakes`
- **P-02** Identify who scores the proposal (admissions board, funder panel, advisor) and what each criterion rewards; write to those criteria explicitly, in their words, section by section. Why: reviewers score against their criteria, not against the author's priorities. → `skills/research-grants/references/review_criteria.md`
- **P-03** Fit with the call is shown, not asserted: quote the exact item of the call (problem number, question, product) the proposal serves, and state honestly which parts it does not serve. Never claim the project "solves" a policy problem it only informs. Why (labelled example, M-n_HTTM 2026-10-16): a report "ứng với" a city product it could only feed into was flagged MAJOR. → core C-27
- **P-04** Every institutional or legal fact (law, decree, circular, consolidated text, exchange rule, effective date, deadline, who is treated) is checked against the primary text with number, date, article, clause and point, and quoted; press reports are leads, not sources. Amendments are traced to the last consolidated version (e.g. TT57 → TT69 → TT139). Unverified facts carry `[CẦN XÁC MINH]` / `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED`. → core C-65 to C-68
- **P-05** Applicant eligibility conditions in the call (full-time study, degree field, publication count, age, language, supervisor approval, employer consent) are checked against the applicant's own sections (CV, study plan); a conflict is MAJOR and is put to the user, never resolved silently. Why: an eligibility contradiction sinks the file regardless of quality. → core C-14
- **P-06** List every document the proposal needs but the session cannot access (paywalled paper, scanned decree, exchange notice, data licence); give the user a complete search list: exact title, author/agency, year, number/DOI, why needed, where used, which part, where to look, search keywords (VN and EN), priority. Repeat the list at the start of every round until supplied. Never replace a missing primary source with a weaker one.

## B. Problem, gap, contribution

- **P-10** Open with the problem and why it matters now, in 2–4 sentences with concrete numbers from verified sources; no generic hook that could open any proposal. → `skills/research-grants/references/specific_aims_guide.md#opening-paragraph-the-hook`
- **P-11** State the gap as "what is known / what is not known / why that matters", each backed by citations to the closest papers; name the 3–5 closest papers and say exactly how this project differs. Why: a gap with no named neighbours reads as unread literature. → `skills/research-grants/references/specific_aims_guide.md#second-paragraph-gap-and-context`; orchestrator O-71
- **P-12** "First study", "chưa có nghiên cứu nào" is allowed only with the search scope stated (databases, keywords, years, languages, date of search); otherwise "to our knowledge, within [scope]". → core C-02
- **P-13** The contribution is one sentence a referee can repeat, then at most three bullets (data, design, measure, setting); each contribution maps to an aim and a product. Incremental work is not presented as transformative. → `skills/research-grants/SKILL.md#conceptual-mistakes`
- **P-14** Literature review is organised by the gap, not author by author; every paper cited was read (core C-05), and the empirical papers the results will be compared with are kept. → orchestrator O-72
- **P-15** Theory or conceptual framework names the mechanism(s) that link treatment or driver to outcome and the competing explanation for each hypothesis; a figure of the framework is optional but every arrow in it must be argued in the text.

## C. Objectives, questions, hypotheses

- **P-20** One general objective plus 2–4 specific objectives (aims). Each aim starts with an action verb, is independently valuable (failure of one does not kill the rest), and is achievable within the time and resources stated. Too many aims is a feasibility FAIL. → `skills/research-grants/references/specific_aims_guide.md#specific-aims-main-section`
- **P-21** Each research question is answerable with the stated data and design; each hypothesis is signed, testable and falsifiable, and states what result would count against it. Topic statements are not hypotheses. → `skills/aer-preregistration/SKILL.md#pre-analysis-plan-contents`; `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 3)
- **P-22** One-to-one chain: question → objective → hypothesis → outcome variable → estimator → data → expected product. A question with no hypothesis or method, or a method with no question, is MAJOR (see consistency matrix P-71).
- **P-23** Wording follows the design (core C-20 to C-27): only the aim with an identifying design may promise an "effect/impact/tác động"; descriptive or associational aims say "đo lường", "so sánh", "liên hệ". The general objective and "đối tượng nghiên cứu" must not promise more causal scope than the causal aim delivers. Why (labelled example, M-n_HTTM r7 MJ8). 
- **P-24** Separate confirmatory from exploratory analyses; estimation-only aims are labelled as such and excluded from the multiple-testing family.

## D. Design, data, identification, power (Seat 2)

- **P-30** For each causal aim write the estimand (ATT, LATE…), the source of variation, the identifying assumption, the threats (anticipation, spillovers, concurrent shocks, selection, attrition) and the test or design feature that addresses each. Use the route card in Seat 2 (S2-27) and `skills/mhe-identification/SKILL.md` (Critic mode). → `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 3)
- **P-31** Estimating equations are fully specified before data are seen: outcome construction, controls, fixed effects, sample window (including excluded transition periods), clustering or inference method, and the decision rule ("H is supported when …"). Phrases like "we will explore", "if appropriate", "as needed" are FAIL unless followed by an operational rule. → `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 1, item 4)
- **P-32** Few treated units or a single event date: inference is planned for that case (randomisation/permutation over placebo dates, Conley-Taber, Ferman-Pinto, wild cluster bootstrap) and sensitivity to parallel-trends violations is pre-specified (Rambachan-Roth). Staggered timing uses a heterogeneity-robust estimator (Callaway-Sant'Anna, Sun-Abraham, de Chaisemartin-D'Haultfœuille, Borusyak et al.). → `skills/mhe-identification/SKILL.md`; `references/methods-gap-fill.md`
- **P-33** Power or MDE is reported for each confirmatory hypothesis with its assumptions (effect benchmark from literature, variance, ICC or serial correlation, attrition, number of periods); if not computable yet, state exactly what will be computed, on which data, before registration. "Not significant" will be reported with CI and MDE. → `skills/statistical-power/SKILL.md`; `skills/aer-preregistration/SKILL.md#power-and-the-minimum-detectable-effect`
- **P-34** Multiple testing: the family, the correction (Holm, Romano-Wolf, BH, index) and the p-value used for each hypothesis are fixed in advance. → `skills/aer-preregistration/SKILL.md`
- **P-35** Composite indices: construction rule fixed ex ante (indicators, normalisation reference period, direction, weights, missing data, sensitivity to weights) following OECD/JRC handbook practice; normalise on a fixed reference period if the index will be updated. → `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 4, item 6)
- **P-36** Data plan per variable: source, provider, coverage years, frequency, access status (confirmed / pending / licence cost), and the fallback if access fails. Data the applicant does not yet have are never described as in hand. → `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 5)
- **P-37** Pre-registration: if the design depends on a future event or new data, register the plan (OSF, AEA, EGAP) with a timestamp before the event or data access; state what is registered and the deviation policy. → `skills/aer-preregistration/SKILL.md#pre-registration-gate`
- **P-38** Contingency plans for each design risk (event delayed or split, control group contaminated, data shorter than planned, index inputs unavailable): what changes and which question is still answered. Number them and keep the count consistent across sections.

## E. Feasibility: timeline, resources, ethics, data management, budget

- **P-40** Timeline (Gantt or table) with milestones and deliverables per aim and per half-year; it matches the program length in the call (e.g. ≤ 48 months) and the data availability dates. → `skills/research-grants/references/core_components.md`
- **P-41** Resources and capability: the applicant's prior outputs, methods training, supervisor, data and software access are stated factually and mapped to the aims they enable; no inflated claims about publications under review. → `skills/research-grants/references/writing_principles.md#integration-and-coherence`
- **P-42** Risk register: risk | probability | consequence | mitigation | which aim is affected. → `skills/research-grants/references/writing_principles.md#addressing-risk-and-feasibility`
- **P-43** Ethics, data protection and licences: human subjects/consent if any, personal data, data-provider terms, open-code and data-sharing plan (what, where, when, under which licence). → `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 5, item 5)
- **P-44** Budget (when required) matches activities line by line; every cost appears in the plan and every planned activity is costed. → `skills/research-grants/assets/budget_justification_template.md`
- **P-45** Coherence check: budget, timeline, team, aims and products describe the same project; contradictions (preliminary data vs gap, claimed expertise vs record, aims vs methods) are MAJOR. → `skills/research-grants/references/writing_principles.md#avoid-contradictions`

## F. Expected results, products and impact

- **P-50** Expected results are stated as what will be learned under each outcome (supported, not supported, null with MDE), never as predicted coefficients presented as findings. → core C-01, C-25
- **P-51** Products are concrete and verifiable (dataset, index with code, papers with target journals, policy note), each linked to an aim and a date; policy relevance stays within the tested scope (core C-27).
- **P-52** Scientific, practical and policy contributions are separated; claims about benefit to a city, ministry or market name the user of the result and the decision it informs, marked `[CẦN XÁC MINH]` if not confirmed.

## G. Vietnamese PhD proposal conventions (đề cương nghiên cứu sinh)

- **P-60** Follow the institution's template exactly; when none is given use: Tên đề tài; Tính cấp thiết; Tổng quan và khoảng trống; Mục tiêu (tổng quát, cụ thể); Câu hỏi và giả thuyết; Đối tượng và phạm vi; Phương pháp và dữ liệu; Đóng góp dự kiến (khoa học, thực tiễn); Kế hoạch thực hiện; Dự kiến sản phẩm; Kế hoạch học tập; Tài liệu tham khảo; Danh mục chữ viết tắt, bảng, hình.
- **P-61** Title: ≤ 25–30 words, names the phenomenon, the setting and the type of evidence; no slogans; consistent between cover, section 1 and every form.
- **P-62** Academic Vietnamese: clear subject, one idea per sentence, no promotional or bureaucratic filler, terms defined once and used identically (core C-26). Technical terms keep the English in parentheses at first use.
- **P-63** APA 7 citation: inside parentheses use "&" — (Vo & Phan, 2017); in running text use "và" — Vo và Phan (2017); "và cộng sự" / "et al." from three authors; citations and reference list match two ways; Vietnamese and English lists follow the institution's ordering rule. → `scripts/check_crossrefs.py`
- **P-64** Legal documents cited with full number, type, issuing body and date (e.g. Thông tư số 139/2025/TT-BTC ngày 30/12/2025 của Bộ Tài chính); consolidated texts with their VBHN number and date.
- **P-65** No tracked changes in the deliverable unless the user asks; deliver a clean .docx per round with a change log (`nhat-ky-thay-doi-round-N.md`); preserve styles, equations (OMML), SmartArt and the table of contents.
- **P-66** Privacy: before committing proposal files, personal CVs or source PDFs, check whether the repository is private; if public or unknown, ask the user.

## H. Committee review lens (Seat 1)

- **P-70** Score the proposal the way the committee will, with one paragraph each: significance, innovation, approach (rigour, feasibility, alternatives), investigator capability, environment and resources, fit with the call; verdict per criterion with the weakest point first. → `skills/research-grants/references/review_criteria.md`
- **P-71** Consistency matrix (mandatory, Seat 2 builds it): rows = research questions; columns = objective, hypothesis, outcome variable, equation/estimator, data source, test and threshold, expected product, timeline slot, section numbers. Any empty or mismatched cell is a finding. Also check that counts (number of aims, hypotheses, contingency plans, products) are identical everywhere they are stated.
- **P-72** Overclaiming and underclaiming: flag causal or importance claims beyond the design; also flag real strengths the text hides. → `skills/review-pap/SKILL.md` (Agent 3, item 6); `skills/credible-claims/SKILL.md`
- **P-73** Every number, date and citation in the proposal is traced to a source in the reference list or the applicant's documents; numbers from memory are CRITICAL (core C-01, C-04).
- **P-74** Readability for a mixed panel: section overviews, topic sentences, defined abbreviations list, figures with self-explanatory captions; jargon explained once. → `skills/research-grants/references/writing_principles.md#clarity-and-accessibility`

## I. Process for proposals (adapts the orchestrator card)

- **P-80** Phases: P0 intake (call, template, criteria, facts, missing-document list) → P1 question and gap (closest papers verified) → P2 aims, hypotheses, consistency matrix → P3 design, data, power, pre-registration plan → P4 feasibility (timeline, risks, ethics, budget) → P5 draft in the template → P6 scripts and self-audit → P7 two-seat panel loop until no CRITICAL/MAJOR (core C-35) → P8 deliver docx, change log, remaining `[CẦN XÁC MINH]` list and document search list.
- **P-81** Revising from committee or advisor comments: categorise each comment MUST ADDRESS / SHOULD ADDRESS / CONSIDER / DISAGREE, show the table and wait for the user before applying; do not rewrite sections nobody commented on; back up before saving; report changes with section and reason. → `skills/proposal-revise/SKILL.md#step-2-collect-feedback`
- **P-82** A user-chosen title, design decision or scope choice is never reopened by an agent; it can be flagged once with evidence, then the user decides. → core C-14
- **P-83** Search depth: legal and data facts to the primary text and article/clause; literature to the published version (journal, volume, pages, DOI) and the table or page that supports the claim; strategy-level summaries are not evidence. → core C-60 to C-62

## J. Rules from the strongest proposal skills (added 2026-10-27)

- **P-90** Outline approval is a red line: show the outline (sections, aims, hypotheses, design per aim, data, products) and wait for the user before drafting; if the user says "you decide", write down the locked assumptions and carry them into the change log. → `skills/research-proposal/SKILL.md`
- **P-91** Write with verify: draft one section at a time; before moving on, check its in-text citations against the reference list and every quantitative or directional claim against its source. Generating the whole proposal in one pass is FAIL. → `skills/research-proposal/SKILL.md`; `skills/research-proposal/references/CITATION_INTEGRITY.md`
- **P-92** No idea, no draft: without a research spec (question in one sentence, directional hypothesis, identifying assumption and main threat, data, contribution, open questions) stop and run the spec interview (puzzle → theory → data → identification and biggest threat → expected result and what would surprise → contribution), one or two questions per turn. Never invent the identification strategy or aims. → `skills/interview-me/SKILL.md`; `skills/grant-proposal/SKILL.md`
- **P-93** Prose first: sections are connected paragraphs (claim, evidence, explanation, link); lists only for research questions, objectives, hypotheses and timeline milestones; contributions, methods and significance are not bullet lists. → `skills/research-proposal/references/WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE.md`
- **P-94** Length balance unless the template says otherwise: introduction and problem 15–20%, literature and gap 20–25%, methodology 20–25%, significance and contribution 10–15%, timeline 5–10%, abstract 5–10%; for social science and economics the literature review has theoretical framework, empirical review and research gap; methodology has design, data and sampling, analysis, ethics. → `skills/research-proposal/references/STRUCTURE_GUIDE.md`; `skills/research-proposal/references/DOMAIN_TEMPLATES.md`
- **P-95** Name the gap type (knowledge, methodological, population/setting, theoretical) and say why it matters; a gap that is only "no study in Vietnam" needs a reason the setting changes the answer. → `skills/research-proposal/references/STRUCTURE_GUIDE.md`
- **P-96** Reference base: no padding; a PhD proposal typically cites 25–50 verified sources, a large share recent, plus the canonical sources for each method; mark any unverifiable entry `[CẦN XÁC MINH]` and tell the user. → `skills/research-proposal/references/QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`
- **P-97** Methods name the exact estimator and software (e.g. `fixest::feols`, `did::att_gt`, `reghdfe`, `HonestDiD`) and copy the identification block from the spec: estimand, treated and control, assumption, placebo and robustness, clustering. Without preliminary results write "[KẾT QUẢ SƠ BỘ: chưa có — mô tả pilot dự kiến]". → `skills/grant-proposal/SKILL.md`
- **P-98** Coherence artifact: every aim has at least one milestone and one product; every milestone and every method serves an aim; every cost line names its aim; admin or licensed data imply an access plan and cost; confidential data are never promised for public release. → `skills/grant-proposal/SKILL.md`; `skills/data-management-plan/SKILL.md`
- **P-99** Status label: "DRAFT READY" only when every required section of the call is filled and the coherence check passes; otherwise "INCOMPLETE — N items" with each gap marked `[CLARIFY: …]` / `[CẦN XÁC MINH]`. → `skills/grant-proposal/SKILL.md`
- **P-100** Pre-registration checks: refuse to register anything that already contains results; hypotheses directional (a null needs an equivalence test); primary outcome and estimator named; sample size or stopping rule numeric; exclusion and outlier rules ex ante; unit of randomisation or treatment matches the unit of analysis or clustering handles it; MUST/SHOULD/MAY per field, missing MUST means INCOMPLETE. → `skills/preregister/SKILL.md`
- **P-101** Power: fix two of {effect, N, power} and solve the third; never post-hoc power; inputs (estimand, sidedness, α, baseline mean/SD, allocation, ICC or serial correlation, number of tests) come from data, pilot or literature, never guessed; DEFF = 1 + (m − 1)ρ; DiD/IV power by simulation with the real estimator, checking size under the null first; report MDE in raw and standardised units with a paste-ready methods paragraph. → `skills/power-analysis/SKILL.md`
- **P-102** Banned LLM tells in proposal prose (Vietnamese equivalents included): "In recent years", "It is worth noting", "plays a pivotal role", "delves into", "garnered significant attention", "Trong bối cảnh hiện nay", "Có thể nói rằng", "đóng vai trò then chốt", "ngày càng thu hút sự quan tâm"; hedge to the evidence ("nhằm", "dự kiến", "có thể") and never "will prove". → `skills/research-proposal/references/WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE.md`; `scripts/check_docx_style.py`
- **P-103** Decision records: when there is a real alternative (DiD vs synthetic control, admin vs survey data, panel vs event study), write a short decision record (options, criteria, choice, why, what would change it) and keep it with the proposal. → `skills/interview-me/SKILL.md`
- **P-104** Call record before drafting: call ID and amendment, deadline with time zone, eligibility, form version, limits, criteria, URL and retrieval date; an archived call does not prove the scheme is open; page limits are checked on the exported final file, not on a draft. → `skills/research-grants/SKILL.md`
