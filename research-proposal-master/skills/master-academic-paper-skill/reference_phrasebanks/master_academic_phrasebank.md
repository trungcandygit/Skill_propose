## 1. Introducing Work & Establishing Significance

### Highlighting Real-World Importance & Empirical Stakes
- A central question in [field] concerns the extent to which [X] influences [Y].
- Understanding the determinants of [phenomenon] is critical for both theoretical asset pricing and practical risk management.
- Recent turbulent market episodes have renewed interest in the mechanisms governing [X].
- The economic consequences of [policy/shock] remain a subject of intense debate among economists and policymakers.

### Framing the Theoretical or Practical Tension
- While classical theory predicts that [X] should lead to [Y], empirical evidence remains sharply divided.
- A fundamental tension exists between [mechanism A], which implies [outcome A], and [mechanism B], which suggests [outcome B].
- The existing theoretical literature offers ambiguous predictions regarding the sign of [parameter].

## 2. Identifying Research Gaps & Problematizing Prior Art

### Highlighting Methodological or Identification Limitations
- While several studies examine the correlation between [X] and [Y], establishing a causal link remains challenging due to [endogeneity/reverse causality/omitted variable bias].
- Existing empirical work largely relies on low-frequency aggregated data, which masks the rapid intraday transmission of [shock].
- Previous estimates may be confounded by [specific institutional friction], which simultaneously affects both treatment and outcome.

### Highlighting Conflicting or Inconclusive Findings
- Despite extensive inquiry, empirical evidence on [topic] remains contradictory: while [Author A] finds [result A], [Author B] documents [result B].
- Much of the existing evidence is derived from mature Western economies, leaving open the question of whether these dynamics generalize to emerging frontier markets.
- Little is known about how [shock] propagates under conditions of severe liquidity constraints.

- The inclusion of granular industry-by-year fixed effects (Column 3) leaves the point estimate virtually unchanged, suggesting that our findings are not driven by unobserved sector-specific trends.

- The first-stage Kleibergen-Paap Wald $F$-statistic of 38.4 comfortably exceeds the Stock-Yogo critical value for weak instruments.
